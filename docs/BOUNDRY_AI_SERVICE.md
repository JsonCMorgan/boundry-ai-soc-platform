# Boundry.AI — Security Monitoring Service

## What We Do
We protect small businesses from cyberattacks by monitoring their web applications 24/7 and delivering plain-English incident reports when something goes wrong.

---

## The Problem
Most small businesses have no idea when they're being attacked. Hackers don't announce themselves — they probe quietly, guess passwords, and steal data while you're focused on running your business. By the time you notice, the damage is done.
