What you are doing here

Each case is one situation you handled like a Tier-1 analyst: what you saw, why it mattered, what you decided, and what you would do next. Recruiters see the PDF export, not raw SIEM noise.

  • One case = one story. Example: password spray on a VPN, phishing report, or a finding you closed as false positive.
  • Write for a hiring manager. Plain English. Name the alert, 1–3 facts from logs, and your decision.
  • MITRE is optional. Add a technique ID only if it fits (e.g. T1110 for password guessing).

Two ways cases get here

  • Quick Shift: Finish a shift with good ticket notes and Boundry can auto-add a case.
  • Add below: You document a drill, lab alert, or practice scenario by hand.
