🛡️ Security Settings

Analyst-only controls for hardening the Boundry.AI command center itself.

🚦 Login Rate Limit

Caps how many login attempts a single IP can make per minute. Raising the strictness (Lockdown) is the standard containment step when you spot account enumeration, brute-force, or credential-stuffing activity. Changes take effect immediately — no restart — and every attempt that gets throttled is logged to your SIEM feed.

Scope: this throttle protects this platform's own login (the Boundry.AI command center). It does not reach a client's separate website or login portal on their own infrastructure — that traffic never passes through here.
